I welcome inquiries from motivated students and researchers interested in cannabis science, evidence synthesis, and related public-health questions.

## Who should reach out

- Graduate students seeking thesis or dissertation supervision aligned with the lab’s themes
- Undergraduate researchers looking for structured project experience
- Collaborators building open research tooling or literature workflows

## How to apply

Email a brief note of interest to [miladn1@mcmaster.ca](mailto:miladn1@mcmaster.ca) with:

1. Your current program or role and institution
2. A short paragraph on research interests and relevant experience
3. A CV or résumé (PDF preferred)

## Shared research tooling

Fellow cannabis researchers may also find the [Cannabis Research Intelligence Tool](https://cannabis-paper-scraper.fly.dev) useful for literature discovery and monitoring.
